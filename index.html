04 / THINKING

How I think

01

Measure the bottleneck first

Before automating a process, I assess its time cost, error rate, and operational overhead. Then I choose the simplest approach that addresses the bottleneck.

02

Own the last mile

A feature is not finished when a pull request is merged. Deployment, observability, documentation, and handover all matter in production.

03

Choose simplicity deliberately

When two approaches meet the requirements, I favor the simpler one that the team can understand, maintain, and extend.

Now
